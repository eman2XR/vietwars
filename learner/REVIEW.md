# Progress review, 2026-11-22

## Where things stand

- Six sessions in two days: four issues, one review, one issue closed midway. Played every day.
- 46 words met, 9 of them on the known list; 32 hold up by meaning (0.8 or more). The vocabulary phase aims at about 300, so roughly an eighth of the way.
- Spaced reviews: one short review so far (2.5 minutes, 63%: triệu right; mươi, lăm and rưỡi half right), so there is too little data on retention yet.
- Hearing words is strong: listening 82%, rush hour 44 of 45 across the issues. Prices in real life are the weakest skill: 60% right (9 of 15), at about 27 seconds an answer.
- The stories now land: issue 3 "Skipped it" (14 seconds on the opening), issue 4 "Interesting" (40 seconds), after the story was spread through the issue with pictures.
- Difficulty is right: issues 3 and 4 "Just right", fun "Good" both times, 81% and 76% first try.

## What is working / what is not

- Working: the magazine format and the integrated stories; relaxed typing; listening practice; pictures (the learner asked for more, and issue 4 had them everywhere).
- Not working: the number words. trăm failed 8 of 13 tries, mươi 5 of 11, rưỡi 6 of 9, lăm 5 of 8, triệu 4 of 8, and the worst single item is a price, một trăm linh năm (2 of 2 missed, once typed as 10000). Most misses are of meaning, not spelling: triệu taken for a thousand twice, trăm and nghìn both typed as "trien".
- Typing from memory: 34% strictly right, 66% counting letters-right answers; 21 of 62 typed answers were another word. It is the slowest part of every issue.
- Length: issue 4 was about 20 minutes of play (the 120 minutes recorded include one 100-minute break), the first attempt stopped at step 33 of 83, and the learner wrote "The lesson is a bit too long, should be shorter."
- The "I know it" button has never been used, so the known list stays as it is.

## Changes made

- Next unit: market, refocused as "prices as sellers say them", moved ahead of politeness. The learner asked for exactly this ("someone telling me a price and me understanding what it is", with nghìn usually dropped and hundreds shortened), and it consolidates the weakest words. Every spoken form must be confirmed by the Vietnamese reviewer.
- Issues aim at about 45 answers (was 55), and the checker warns above 50: issue 4 felt too long at 58.
- Review words are no longer typed inside issues, only heard and used; typing them belongs to the spaced reviews. Typing the same number words in issue 4 failed every time. This also shortens issues by the number of review words.
- New notes for triệu and lăm, and earlier today for trăm, nghìn and nữa, that set the number words apart; reviews show notes. The mươi hook from the battle version (the Fat Cat) was replaced.
- Every issue gets at least five price lines (rule added to docs/PEDAGOGY.md).

## Suggestions for the learner

- Do the review desk when it lights up: two or three minutes a day is where number words get typed and come back at the right time.
- In real-life questions, tap Slow and say the price aloud before answering.
- If a spoken price confuses you (for example "trăm rưỡi"), ask it under Ask: the answer comes back with the Vietnamese to play.
