# Học tiếng Việt (repository: vietwars): working instructions

A personal site for learning **Northern (Hà Nội) Vietnamese**, for one learner (the owner), on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is a small magazine: every lesson is an issue with a story from Vietnamese history or culture (a short intro, then beats through the lesson), a fact for every word, drills, real-life listening (prices, bills, replies), a rush-hour round, a stamp and a real-world challenge. The learner can ask the editors questions at any time. Static site in `web/`, deployed to GitHub Pages (https://eman2xr.github.io/vietwars/) by `.github/workflows/pages.yml` on every push to `main`. Repository: https://github.com/eman2XR/vietwars (public).

## The loop

1. The learner reads and practises an issue, or a review. Every answer, timing, hint, replay, tone confusion, typed answer, real-life score, story reading time and their end-of-issue feedback is saved on the device and posted as a GitHub issue (`web/js/sync.js`, using a fine-grained token with Issues read and write, entered in Settings on each device). Every device reads those issues back on open and rebuilds progress from them, so devices stay in sync (no token needed to read; the repository is public). The Ask page has two parts: Ask a question (a live voice conversation, see `talk.js`) and Feedback for the editors (a written note, posted labelled `feedback`, no answer expected). `ingest.py` saves notes to `learner/feedback/` and conversation transcripts to `learner/talks/`, and both appear in `learner/SUMMARY.md`; treat them as feedback when planning. Typed questions answered as issue comments (`scripts/questions.py`) are retired; old ones stay in `learner/questions.json`.
2. `python3 scripts/ingest.py` saves new results to `learner/results/` and rebuilds `learner/profile.json` and `learner/SUMMARY.md` (meaning and spelling strength per word, known words, warm-up misses, typing, story and real-life signals, struggle scores, feedback, questions asked, and `learner/NOTES.md`).
3. `python3 scripts/status.py --decide` says what to do: `answer-questions`, `ingest`, `review-progress`, `adjust-lesson`, `next-lesson` or `wait`. It keeps exactly one unplayed lesson waiting, at most two new lessons a day, and a progress review every five sessions or seven days.
4. Skills in `.claude/skills/`: `next-lesson` (plan, research, write, review with three subagents, test, publish), `review-progress` (trends, curriculum and rule changes, `learner/REVIEW.md`), `lesson-loop` (one round of the above, answering the learner's questions first; scheduled a few times a day, see the skill's "Starting a loop").

Picking up the work in a new session: run `python3 scripts/status.py`, read `learner/SUMMARY.md`, `learner/NOTES.md` (what the owner said in conversation, newest first) and the top of `docs/LESSON_LOG.md`, then check the loop with CronList: it is a durable scheduled job (in `.claude/scheduled_tasks.json`, not committed) that comes back whenever Claude Code starts in this folder and renews itself every six days, as the `lesson-loop` skill describes; if it is missing, create it as the skill says. Everything else lives in this repository.

Agents may publish lessons on their own (the owner asked for this on 2026-11-21), but only issues that pass every check and all three reviews.

## Layout

- `web/index.html`, `web/style.css` (six styles named after Vietnamese materials, as CSS variables on `[data-style]`), `web/sw.js` (offline cache), `web/manifest.webmanifest`, `web/icons/`.
- `web/js/main.js` (routing, the magazine home with the passport of stamps, words, settings and the style picker, saving and replaying results, syncing between devices, known words), `lesson.js` (the issue engine: opening, story beats in file order, drills, hints, conversation, real life, rush hour, results and stamps, save and resume, the ? question sheet), `vi.js` (tones, colouring, grading, Telex, the on-screen tone keys), `srs.js` (Leitner intervals), `store.js` (localStorage), `audio.js` (clips, in-memory preload, speech fallback), `sync.js` (posting and reading result, question and talk issues, and answers), `talk.js` and `pcm-capture.js` (Ask a question: a live voice conversation with Gemini Live, called Talk in the code, told what is on screen and what the learner knows; the Gemini key is entered per device in Settings or with a one-time link `#/settings?gemini=KEY`, never committed; transcripts are posted as issues labelled `talk`), `pictures.js` (word pictures, diagrams and cover scenes, drawn in one inked style: a colour field, a paper cut-out and dip-pen lines, coloured by the active style).
- `web/lessons/index.json`, `lesson-NN.json` (content only), `known.json` (words the learner knows). `web/audio/`: clips named by a hash of their text, listed in `audio/index.json`.
- `scripts/make_audio.py` (macOS `say -v Linh`), `check_lessons.py` (format, dialect, known words, pictures, stories, situations, story order and length through `plan.mjs`, audio), `check_site.py` (WebKit play-through on iPhone 15, iPhone SE, iPad Pro portrait and landscape, desktop Safari, including asking a question, reading an answer and a second device syncing; screenshots in `.checks/`), `style_shots.py` (a sheet per style in `docs/styles/`), `picture_sheet.py` (every picture in every style, for checking new art), `make_icons.py`, `ingest.py` (also saves Talk transcripts to `learner/talks/`), `status.py`, `outcomes.py` (the recent issues side by side: ratings, minutes, accuracy per exercise, misses; read before planning), `questions.py` (open questions; posting an answer), `plan.mjs` (how an issue plays: answers before rush hour and where each story beat lands), `push.mjs` (notifications to the Home Screen app: `inbox` saves devices, `send` notifies them), `reviews.mjs` (which words are due for review, rebuilt from the results with the app's own `web/js/deck.js`; `notify` sends at most one reminder a day).
- `learner/`: results, profile, summary, `questions.json` (questions and answers), `NOTES.md` (what the learner said outside the app), `curriculum.json` (phases and planned units; agents keep it current), `REVIEW.md` (latest progress review).
- `docs/PEDAGOGY.md` (teaching rules), `docs/LESSON_FORMAT.md` (lesson and result formats), `docs/LESSON_LOG.md` (why each lesson is the way it is), `docs/STYLES.md`.
- `tests/`: `node --test` unit tests for grading, Telex, tone placement, the tone keys and spaced repetition.

## Verify

```sh
node --test
python3 scripts/check_lessons.py
/Library/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.13/bin/python3 scripts/check_site.py
```

`check_site.py` and `style_shots.py` need the Framework Python (it has Playwright and WebKit). Look at the screenshots after visual changes. Run `scripts/make_audio.py` after any change to Vietnamese text. The iOS Simulator works from this sandbox with `DEVELOPER_DIR=/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer` and `$DEVELOPER_DIR/usr/bin/simctl` (boot a device, `simctl openurl booted <url>`, `simctl io booted screenshot <file>`); it can only open the deployed site, since the sandbox cannot serve local files.

## Rules

- Northern dialect only, in words, glosses, tips, articles and audio. Every new issue gets three independent reviews before it ships: Vietnamese accuracy, learning design, and a history and culture fact-check.
- Articles and facts must be true and sourced (`feature.sources`); English and Vietnamese Wikipedia are reachable from the sandbox. Wikimedia Commons images are not reachable yet; until they are, illustrations are original SVG in `web/js/pictures.js`.
- Audio is the macOS voice Linh, used with the owner's agreement for this personal site. Its hỏi tone falls like huyền, so lessons avoid hỏi against huyền contrasts.
- Never teach or review a word on the known list. Judge meaning and spelling separately.
- All art is original and in context: drawings belong to the word or the story (a phở stall, a market), never random animals or mascots. No characters, names, art or lines from existing films, games or books.
- The site must work in Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac and as a Home Screen app: tap targets of at least 44 px, safe-area padding, inputs of 16 px or more, audio started from a tap, no hover-only controls. The Content-Security-Policy in `web/index.html` allows only the site itself, `blob:` audio and `api.github.com`; no inline scripts or styles (set styles from JavaScript through `element.style`).
- Keep result formats readable by `ingest.py`; bump `schema` when a field changes meaning.
- Keep `SHELL_FILES` in `web/sw.js` current when app files are added or removed. The Pages build stamps `web/sw.js` with the commit, so every deploy installs a new offline cache and an open page reloads once onto the new version; the worker always revalidates app files, so a page never mixes two versions.
- Result issues are public (the repository is public). They hold only lesson data and feedback.
- Notifications: a device turns them on in Settings (in the Home Screen app on iPhone and iPad) and posts its push subscription as an issue, sealed with the inbox key in `web/push.json`. `scripts/push.mjs inbox` (run by ingest) saves it to `.push/subscriptions.json` and sends a welcome notification; publishing an issue or answering a question sends one (see the skills). The private keys are in `.push/keys.json` on this Mac only: never commit or print them. If they are lost, run `node scripts/push.mjs init`, publish the new `web/push.json`, and turn notifications on again on each device.
- Commit with the repository's local git identity; signing is off for this repository only. Never force-push.
